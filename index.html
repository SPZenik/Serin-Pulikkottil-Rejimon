About

My work sits at the intersection of animal science and data science: taking beef production systems apart into their feed, emissions, water, and economic loops, and rebuilding them as models that a producer or policymaker can actually act on. That means predicting enteric methane and manure emissions with machine learning, tracing causal loops through a carbon-neutral cattle operation, and building decision-support dashboards that survive contact with real farm data.

I hold a long-standing research affiliation with both Texas A&M and the University of Pretoria's Precision Livestock Farming program, and I work across Python, Vensim system dynamics, and document/reporting pipelines to move research from dataset to publication to something a collaborator can open and use.

Research focus

Six threads, one supply chain

Precision livestock farming

Sensor and data-driven decision support for day-to-day herd management.

Methane & GHG emissions modeling

Predicting enteric methane, manure N₂O, and soil carbon sequestration from herd and diet data.

Machine learning in animal science

Model comparison and SHAP interpretability on livestock and emissions datasets.

Nitrogen use efficiency

Decision support for nitrogen utilization in Gulf watershed beef systems.

Carbon accounting

State-level GHG baselines for Texas beef grazing systems.

System dynamics

Causal loop diagrams and Vensim models of cattle population, feed efficiency, and emissions.

ML pipeline

South African beef cattle GHG modeling

Three models predicting enteric CH₄, manure N₂O, and soil carbon sequestration from a 10,000-record, 41-variable dataset, delivered as a Streamlit dashboard and a standalone web dashboard with 16 IPCC equations and mitigation sliders for 3-NOP, Asparagopsis, and Moringa.

With Srikar Veeranki, Hiya Sharma, Keshav Vidjeabhaskaran
ML pipeline

Methane yield prediction (CH₄/DMI)

Seven models tested on a 251-sample literature dataset with SHAP interpretability; Gradient Boosting performed best (test R² = 0.54), with NDF, DMI, and measurement method as the top drivers.

Systems paper

A carbon-neutral, 1,000-cow beef operation

A systems thinking paper built on causal loop diagrams — seven reinforcing loops and one balancing loop (carbon market), plus four system archetypes, framed as a 25-year narrative.
